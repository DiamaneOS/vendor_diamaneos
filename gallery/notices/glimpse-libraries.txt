Libraries bundled in Glimpse, the DiamaneOS gallery

Glimpse (org.lineageos.glimpse) is LineageOS's gallery, Apache-2.0. It ships
these libraries as prebuilt jars from its own source tree:

- AndroidX Media3 1.10.0 (The Android Open Source Project): Apache-2.0
- Glide 5.0.5 with its disklrucache and gifdecoder (Google, Inc.; Jake
  Wharton and The Android Open Source Project; Xcellent Creations, Inc.):
  BSD-style licence, Apache-2.0 and MIT, in Glide's LICENSE
- Material Components for Android 1.14.0-alpha10 (The Android Open Source
  Project): Apache-2.0
- Okio 3.16.4 (Square, Inc.): Apache-2.0
- ZoomImage 1.4.0 (panpf): Apache-2.0
- Adobe XMPCore 6.1.11 (Adobe Systems Incorporated): BSD-3-Clause

The licence texts are in the device's third-party licence notices.
